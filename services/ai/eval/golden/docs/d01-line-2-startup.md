# Line 2 start-up procedure

This procedure is used at the start of every shift on Line 2 (500 ml and 330 ml PET bottles).

## Before you start

Walk the whole line first. All guards must be closed, every emergency stop must be released, and there must be no tools or loose parts on the conveyor.

Check the air supply. The main air regulator next to the filler must read 6.0 bar before the line is started. If it reads less, do not start; call maintenance on extension 4410.

Check the CO2 bank gauge. If the pressure is below the specification on the gauge label, do not start the filler; call maintenance on extension 4410.

The rinser water pressure must be 2.5 bar.

## Starting

Start the conveyor at 180 bottles per minute. After 10 minutes of stable running, ramp up to 420 bottles per minute.

Take 5 caps from the first run and check the capping torque on the hand torque tester before you continue.

The first 20 bottles after start-up are rejected and go to the waste bin next to the labeler.

## After starting

Sign the start-up log on the HMI screen under "Shift start". The shift lead checks the log within the first hour.
