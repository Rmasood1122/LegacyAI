# Quality sampling on Line 2

Take 5 bottles every 30 minutes from the outfeed of the capper.

| Check | Specification |
|---|---|
| Fill level (500 ml) | 500 ml ± 5 ml |
| Cap removal torque | between 1.2 and 2.0 Nm |
| Label position | within 2 mm of the mark |

If a sample is out of specification, hold the pallet in production with a yellow HOLD tag and tell the shift lead and the quality lab.

Record every sample in the quality log, including the time and the line speed.
