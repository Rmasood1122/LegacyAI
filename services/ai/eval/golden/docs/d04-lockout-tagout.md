# Lock-out / tag-out (LOTO) — Line 2

Use this before any work inside a guard, on a drive, or on the electrical or pneumatic system.

1. Tell the operator of the machine and the shift lead that you are locking out.
2. Stop the machine with the normal stop button.
3. Switch off the main disconnect in electrical cabinet E3.
4. Fit your personal red lock to the disconnect and attach a tag with your name and the date.
5. Release the stored air pressure by opening valve V-9 on the pneumatic panel.
6. Verify zero energy: press the start button. Nothing may move. If anything moves, stop and call the maintenance lead.

## Removing locks

Only the person who fitted a lock may remove it.

If a key is lost, the lock may be cut only with the shift lead and the plant manager both present as witnesses, and the event is recorded in the LOTO register.
