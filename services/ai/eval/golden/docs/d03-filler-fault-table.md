# Filler fault table — Line 2

**F12 — Low bowl level.** Check the product supply valve PV-3 and the level in the product tank. Do not override the level switch.

**F21 — Bottle jam at the infeed.** Stop the infeed, clear the jammed bottles, reset the fault at the panel and restart in jog mode before switching back to automatic.

**F33 — CO2 pressure low.** The alarm comes when the CO2 pressure falls below 3.2 bar. Switch the supply to CO2 bank B and tell the shift lead so that bank A can be refilled.

**F40 — Filling valve leak.** Isolate the valve whose number is shown on the panel. At most 2 valves may be isolated at the same time; if a third valve leaks, stop the filler and call maintenance.

**F51 — Product temperature high.** If the product temperature is above 6 °C, hold filling and call quality before restarting.

**F60 — Door open.** A guard door is open. Close it; the fault resets automatically.
